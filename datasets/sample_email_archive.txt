Hi Team,

Following up on the shift notes from this morning across Area A and Hydrocracking Unit 3.

1. **Feed Pump P-102**: Mechanical seal leakage and pressure drops on P-102 were confirmed by Meera to be caused by backpressure from upstream Valve V-101. Clearing Valve V-101 resolved the pressure drop.

2. **Main Gas Compressor C-201**: High lube oil temperature (>85°C) and high-frequency vibration spike (+24.5%) occurred. We flushed Lube Oil Heat Exchanger E-204 and inspected the seal gas filter, restoring normal bearing temperatures.

3. **Hydrocracker Reactor R-301**: Thermal runaway bed temperature differential exceeded 12°C delta. Emergency H2 quench was injected via Control Valve HCV-304 while throttling the feed preheater duty.

4. **Crude Distillation Column T-401**: Overhead pressure surge (+19.8%) and tray flooding occurred. Increased reflux flow rate on Pump P-402 and purged non-condensable off-gas.
